# ASI External Agreement

> [!abstract] Goal
> Measure how far the ASI agrees with each other appraisal of the same states, with n and an interval, and say beforehand what agreement to expect.

## Why it exists

Concurrent validity: an index of stability should rank states broadly as other careful appraisals do, and its disagreements should be explainable.

## Precise objectives

1. Spearman's rho between the ASI and each appraisal across the 54 states, with its Fisher interval and n.
2. Expected signs, stated now: positive with HDI, IIAG, WGI and CPIA; negative with FSI, INFORM and GPI (where a higher score means worse).
3. The two-correlation z test between editions.
4. Read with the restricted-range caveat: an Africa-only sample lowers correlations.

## Inputs and outputs

- **In:** the ASI and the appraisals in [ASI Appraisals](<../ASI 06 Outputs and Site/ASI Appraisals.md>)
- **Out:** an agreement table, also shown on the site

## Where it stands

Planned (plan 4.9). Edition 1 matched 4 of 5 top and 3 of 5 bottom states against IIAG 2023.
