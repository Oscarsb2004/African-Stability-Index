# ASI Validation

> [!abstract] Goal
> Show three things about the index: it reproduces exactly, it agrees with other appraisals where it should, and it says something about what happens next.

## Why it exists

An index nobody can rebuild, that disagrees with everyone, or that predicts nothing, informs nobody.

## Precise objectives

1. Reproduction: [ASI Verification Layers](<ASI Verification Layers.md>) gate every release.
2. Agreement: [ASI External Agreement](<ASI External Agreement.md>) compares the ASI with other appraisals.
3. Prediction: [ASI Backtests](<Backtests/ASI Backtests.md>) test it against later events.

## Inputs and outputs

- **In:** the panel, other appraisals, later events
- **Out:** pass or fail per layer; agreement and backtest results

## Where it stands

Reproduction is live; agreement is planned; the backtests are pre-registered (2026-10-18).
