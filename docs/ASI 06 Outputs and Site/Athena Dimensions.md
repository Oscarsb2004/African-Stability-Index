# Athena Dimensions

> [!abstract] Goal
> Describe any country across many dimensions, each a partly independent workstream that improves gradually and individually, through consultations with professionals in its field.

## Why it exists

The owner, 2026-10-18: the system is growing to cover many dimensions of any country. Each dimension should be
thought of as partly an independent project, improved over time with advice from people who work in that field,
without splitting the project apart (P64). A dimension is country-agnostic: the ASI applies them to the 54 African
Union states; the Canada atlas, or any other project, can apply them elsewhere.

## Precise objectives

1. Every dimension has a charter: the questions it answers, what it measures, its maturity, the experts to ask and what to ask them, and its backlog.
2. Every dimension improves on its own schedule; a review in one never waits on another.
3. Every change that follows advice is traceable to the consultation that prompted it.
