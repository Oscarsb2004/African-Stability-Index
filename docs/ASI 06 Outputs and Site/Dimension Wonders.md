# Dimension Wonders

> [!abstract] Goal
> Show any country's absolute highlights, natural and human-made, with photographs worth opening full screen.

## Why it exists

The owner's fourth section (P61): like wonders of the world, but not only those.

## Precise objectives

1. Answer these questions for any country: What is most remarkable here, and what does it look like?
2. Move up one maturity level at a time ([Expert Consultation](<../ASI 08 Status and Roadmap/Expert Consultation.md>)), each move earned by a review.
3. Every measure comes from a publisher whose terms are read; nothing is flagged by a threshold of our own (P60).

## What it measures

A short list by a stated rule ([Wonders Selection](<Wonders/Wonders Selection.md>)), each with a few licensed photographs in a full-screen viewer
([Wonders Photo Viewer](<Wonders/Wonders Photo Viewer.md>)).

## Maturity

**D1, prototype.** The rule is applied and the viewer built; no professional has reviewed the list yet.

## Expert review

**Who to ask.** A geographer; a heritage professional (a World Heritage site manager); a conservation scientist; a photographer or travel writer.

**Questions to put to them:**
1. Is the selection rule fair to places the world's encyclopaedias under-cover?
2. What balance of natural and cultural wonders feels right, and per country or per region?
3. What makes a photograph right for this: scale, season, people?
4. What should the short text beside each wonder say?

**Consultations so far:** recorded in `60 Private` (names stay private); see [Expert Consultation](<../ASI 08 Status and Roadmap/Expert Consultation.md>).

## Backlog, in order

3. Build the viewer.
