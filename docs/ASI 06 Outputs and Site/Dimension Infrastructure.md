# Dimension Infrastructure

> [!abstract] Goal
> Map what physically holds any country together and connects it to the world: cities, roads, railways, ports and shipping, power and pipelines, its resources and how they leave.

## Why it exists

The owner asked for the Canada atlas's infrastructure ideas applied to Africa, from public datasets; the same layers serve any country.

## Precise objectives

1. Answer these questions for any country: Where do people live? How are places connected, by road, rail and sea? Where is power made? What are the great projects?
2. Move up one maturity level at a time ([Expert Consultation](<../ASI 08 Status and Roadmap/Expert Consultation.md>)), each move earned by a review.
3. Every measure comes from a publisher whose terms are read; nothing is flagged by a threshold of our own (P60).

## What it measures

- Cities: [Infrastructure Cities](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Roads: [Infrastructure Roads](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Rail and ports: [Infrastructure Railways](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- AID projects: [Infrastructure AID Projects](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Ports and export hubs: [Infrastructure Ports and Export Hubs](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Shipping: [Infrastructure Shipping Routes](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Mines: [Infrastructure Mines and Mineral Facilities](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Resources, output and sales: [Infrastructure Resources Output and Sales](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Grid: [Infrastructure Electricity Grid](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Pipelines: [Infrastructure Pipelines](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- National data: [Infrastructure National Data](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Export chains: [Infrastructure Export Chains](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)
- Megaprojects: [Infrastructure Megaprojects](<ASI Infrastructure Section.md#the-layers>)

Most layers' sources cover the world; AID, the USGS compilation and the AICD cover Africa only.

## Maturity

**D1, prototype, for cities only;** the other layers are D0.

## Expert review

**Who to ask.** A transport or infrastructure planner; a GIS and remote-sensing specialist; an energy analyst; a ports and logistics professional.

**Questions to put to them:**
1. Which layers tell a reader the most about a country's connectivity, and at which zoom?
2. How reliable are AI-detected roads in rural areas you know?
3. What makes a project a "megaproject" worth showing, and how would you choose them?
4. Which sources do practitioners trust for railways, ports and power plants?

**Consultations so far:** recorded in `60 Private` (names stay private); see [Expert Consultation](<../ASI 08 Status and Roadmap/Expert Consultation.md>).
