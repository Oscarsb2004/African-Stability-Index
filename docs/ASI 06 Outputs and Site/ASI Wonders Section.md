# ASI Wonders Section

> [!abstract] Goal
> Show the absolute highlights of the continent, natural and human-made, each with a few photographs the reader can open full screen (P61).

## Why it exists

The owner asked for a non-jarring map of Africa's most amazing features: mountains, rainforests, reserves, historic sites. "Like wonders of the world, but not necessarily."

## Precise objectives

1. A short list chosen by a stated rule ([Wonders Selection](<Wonders/Wonders Selection.md>)).
2. A full-screen photo viewer ([Wonders Photo Viewer](<Wonders/Wonders Photo Viewer.md>)).
3. Every photograph carries its own licence and credit.
4. The languages of each state, and of the continent: official languages (Wikidata) and the languages still spoken (Glottolog), with family, endangerment and description; languages counted, never speakers (P105).

## Inputs and outputs

- **In:** Wikidata, the UNESCO World Heritage List, Wikimedia Commons, Glottolog
- **Out:** the Wonders view

## Where it stands

Built (athena-core #17): pins, a list, and the viewer. Languages added in athena-core #35: Cameroon 294 living languages, 117 endangered; the continent 2,133.
