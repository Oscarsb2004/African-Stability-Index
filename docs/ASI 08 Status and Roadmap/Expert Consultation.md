# Expert Consultation

> [!abstract] Goal
> Improve each dimension through structured consultations with professionals in its field, recorded so every change can be traced to the advice behind it.

## Why it exists

A data system judged only by its builder repeats the builder's blind spots. The ASI's advisors (a data scientist, a
microfinance expert, a Gates Foundation professional and others, ASI advisor notes 2026-09-06 (athena-data)) and the later
reviews changed edition 2 more than any statistic did. The owner can find people in any field over time (P64).

## Precise objectives

1. **Prepare:** a one-page brief per dimension from its charter: scope, measures, a screenshot, the questions.
2. **Consult:** ask the dimension's questions; let the expert add their own.
3. **Record:** a note in `60 Private` per consultation (date, field, advice, in the expert's words where possible); names never leave that folder.
4. **Rule:** advice becomes backlog items; the owner decides what is adopted, in Decisions (athena-data).
5. **Advance:** the dimension's maturity moves only when its criteria are met.
6. **Widen:** ask each expert who else is worth speaking to (Advisors and Contributors (athena-data)).

## Maturity levels

| Level | Name | Reached when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D0 | Charter | the questions, measures and experts are written down |
| D1 | Prototype | built on public data whose terms are read, with no outside review yet |
| D2 | Reviewed | at least one professional in the field has reviewed what is measured and how it is shown, and every point has an answer |
| D3 | Revised | the agreed changes are made and a second professional has reviewed the result |
| D4 | Maintained | published, with an update schedule and someone to consult at each edition |

## Inputs and outputs

- **In:** a dimension's charter; the experts' time
- **Out:** consultation notes, rulings, a higher maturity

## Where it stands

Live. One round so far: the ASI's advisors (2026-09-06) and the reviews that followed.
