# ASI Methods

> [!abstract] Goal
> Turn raw indicators into comparable scores by rules stated in advance, each with its evidence and a test that would show it wrong.

## Why it exists

Every method choice moves scores. The plan's section 4 lists twelve issues found in edition 1, each with what peer
indices do (INFORM, FSI, GPI, IIAG, OECD, WGI, ND-GAIN, the JRC audits) and a first position the owner approved (P49).
The statistics follow the owner's ADA course (P46).

## Precise objectives

1. Every rule is written before it is applied, and its effect on scores is reported step by step.
2. Every statistic carries its n; every score its interval.
3. No method is tuned until a check passes.

## Inputs and outputs

- **In:** the panel of indicators per state and year
- **Out:** pillar and composite scores, intervals, tiers

## Where it stands

Designed and approved; built one step at a time in [ASI Edition 2](<../ASI 01 Goals and Rulings/ASI Edition 2.md>).
