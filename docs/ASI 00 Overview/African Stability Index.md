# African Stability Index

> [!abstract] Goal
> Measure how stable each of the 54 African Union member states is, by a stated method that anyone can rebuild, and show it beside every other credible appraisal of the same countries.

## Why it exists

The owner's flagship project (P43). Stability follows Interpeace's *Rethinking Stability* (P47): a positive,
inclusive peace resting on the conditions people need, with a legitimate state that represents and protects
everyone. The index is one appraisal among several (P53), presented on a satellite atlas with four sections (P55).

## Precise objectives

1. Every published score rebuilds exactly from Athena's datasets ([ASI Verification Layers](<../ASI 05 Verification/ASI Verification Layers.md>)).
2. Every figure carries its n and interval, and countries are grouped in tiers, not 54 point ranks (P46).
3. Pillars overlap as little as theory allows and reach as many sources as possible (P52, P57).
4. The index is tested against what happened next ([ASI Backtests](<../ASI 05 Verification/Backtests/ASI Backtests.md>)).
5. The site shows the ASI beside the other appraisals, an economic outlook, infrastructure and the continent's wonders.

## Inputs and outputs

- **In:** Athena's datasets (WDI and WGI today; UCDP, IMF WEO, HDR and others next)
- **Out:** scores, tiers and bands per state and year; the atlas site; methodology

## Where it stands

Edition 1 is live (the legacy Dash site, frozen pull of 2026-08-08) and reproduces exactly from Athena. Edition 2 is
being built. The atlas prototype and shared sections are in athena-core #15.
